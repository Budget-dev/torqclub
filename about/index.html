Who we are

A Fitness Community Built Around Progress.

TORQ CLUB is a premium fitness and training community for people who want to move with purpose.

We bring together thoughtful coaching, quality training spaces, and a culture of consistency. Whether you are starting your fitness journey or working toward a new performance goal, we believe your training should challenge you while helping you grow.

We are here for the early mornings, the difficult sessions, the small victories, and the moments when you realize how far you have come.
